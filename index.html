ABOUT THIS DEMO

A starting point, built to learn.

Girls’ Future Lab is an early demonstration of a proposed accessible learning platform. It shows example topic cards, sample lessons, and a simple rule-based question-and-answer interaction.

This is not a completed or pilot-tested product. It does not collect personal information, have user accounts, or use live AI. Health material is general education and does not replace professional advice.
