ABOUT GIRLS’ FUTURE LAB

A starting point for learning and growth.

Girls’ Future Lab is an early, open-source educational demonstration designed around practical learning for adolescent girls. The current version contains sample lessons, short quizzes, personal reflection activities, local progress tracking, and prepared question responses.

Privacy by design

No account is needed. Quiz progress and reflections are saved in this browser only; they are not uploaded to a server.

Know the limits

This is an early prototype, not a formally tested or piloted product. It has no live AI, professional counselling, or emergency service.

Get trusted support

For personal health, safety, abuse, or financial concerns, talk to a trusted adult or qualified local professional. Do not share identifying or sensitive details here.

Health lessons provide general education only and do not replace advice from a qualified health professional. Content should be reviewed by qualified health, education, safeguarding, and local community advisers before formal use with adolescents.
